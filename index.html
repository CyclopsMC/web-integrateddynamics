A Minecraft mod to take full and automated control of your appliances

Build networks that read values from the world, reason about them, and act on them. A mix of bundled redstone, logic gates and storage-style networks, with hooks into most other mods.

Download on CurseForge Read the book

The in-game guide, On the Dynamics of Integration, is also readable online.

Requires the Cyclops Core and Common Capabilities libraries.

What it does

  • Readers and writers

    Parts on a cable network read values from the world and write them back: redstone, inventories, fluids, machines, entities and more.

  • Logic Programmer

    Combine values with hundreds of operators, from arithmetic and comparison to map, filter and reduce, and use recipes as first-class values.

  • Variables on the network

    Store values on variable cards, show them on display panels, delay them, or proxy them anywhere on the network.

  • Built for integration

    One network reaches the machines and inventories of other mods. Addons add item transfer, autocrafting, terminals and scripting.
